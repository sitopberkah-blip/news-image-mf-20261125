# The World Tonight — Wednesday 25 November 2026

One image summing up the world news of 25 November 2026, drawn as a
newspaper front page. `main.py` (standard library only) copies the
front-page image `front.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`.

## Stories covered

- **Lead — Houthi drone hits Riyadh's tallest tower:** 11 hurt as the
  PIF Tower is struck for a second time in five days (Reuters, 24 Nov).
- **Huawei guilty in U.S. trial:** a Brooklyn jury convicts on 10 of
  12 counts — trade-secret conspiracy and bank fraud over business in
  Iran and North Korea; racketeering fails (AP / Bloomberg, 24 Nov).
- **Ukraine hits Russian ships:** Zelenskiy confirms Kyiv struck a
  Russian cargo ship in the Gulf of Finland; Ukraine says it also sank
  a Russian warship off Libya (RFE/RL / Reuters, 24 Nov).
- **Poland — Russian drone incursion:** Warsaw says a Russian
  surveillance drone entered its airspace deliberately; a NATO jet was
  cleared to fire on a Russian missile over Poland (Fox News / Reuters).
- **Mine hits ship in Hormuz:** Iran's Guards say a container ship hit
  a mine in the Strait of Hormuz; traffic is down more than 40% this
  month (World Brief Now).
- **Also today:** Trump gives Giuliani the Medal of Freedom; England
  beat Australia by 8 wickets in the 2nd T20I; CIA and ODNI hit by a
  cyber breach; U.S. weighs new tariffs on China and the EU.

All headlines were checked against reputable wire reporting of
24–25 November 2026 before drawing. The image is 1024×1024 and the
program is deterministic: every run writes the same bytes.
